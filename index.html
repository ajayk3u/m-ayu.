First aid guide

Step-by-step guidance for common emergencies

Works offline
CPR / cardiac arrest
▾

Check responsiveness and breathing. If absent, start chest compressions immediately.

  • Push hard and fast in the center of the chest, about 2 inches deep.
  • Maintain a rate of 100–120 compressions per minute.
  • Let the chest fully recoil between compressions.
  • Continue until help arrives or the person responds.

Severe bleeding
▾

Do not remove any embedded object.

  • Press down firmly on the wound with a clean cloth or bare hands.
  • Do not let go — maintain constant pressure.
  • If possible, raise the injured area above heart level.
  • Add more cloth on top if it soaks through; don't remove the first layer.
Burns
▾

No ice, butter, or ointments on a fresh burn.

  • Cool the burn under clean running water for 10–20 minutes.
  • Remove tight clothing or jewelry near the area before it swells.
  • Cover loosely with a clean, non-stick cloth.
  • Seek medical care for large, deep, or facial burns.
Choking
▾

Ask "are you choking?" — if they can't speak or cough, act immediately.

  • Give 5 firm slaps between the shoulder blades.
  • Follow with 5 quick upward abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver).
  • Repeat the 5-and-5 cycle until the object is dislodged.
  • If they become unresponsive, begin CPR.
Seizure
▾

Do not restrain the person or put anything in their mouth.

  • Cushion their head and clear hard or sharp objects away.
  • Time the seizure — call 108 if it lasts longer than 5 minutes.
  • Once movements stop, gently roll them onto their side.
  • Stay with them until they're fully alert.
